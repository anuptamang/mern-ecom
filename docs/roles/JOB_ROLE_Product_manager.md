# Senior Product Manager

## About the Role

As the Senior Product Manager, you will lead and drive product direction for our enterprise e-commerce platform. You will balance business needs, user experience, and technical feasibility to deliver innovative products that meet our customers’ expectations. You will collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, design, and business teams, acting as the voice of the customer and the strategic owner of the product roadmap.

The ideal candidate demonstrates a deep understanding of SaaS and e-commerce platforms, excels at managing multiple priorities, and is highly skilled in collecting and synthesizing feedback to inform product decisions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop, own, and communicate the product vision, strategy, roadmap, and success metrics in alignment with business objectives.
- Gather and prioritize product requirements from customers, stakeholders, data, and competitive intelligence.
- Translate business requirements into clear product specifications and user stories.
- Collaborate closely with design, engineering, operations, and marketing teams throughout the entire product lifecycle.
- Lead product discovery, market research, and validation of new features or solutions.
- Define, track, and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure product success and steer continuous improvement.
- Advocate for exceptional user experience in feature planning and day-to-day decisions.
- Anticipate risks and proactively communicate progress, challenges, and changes to stakeholders.
- Own product backlog and prioritization, ensuring the highest value items are delivered efficiently.
- Stay current with industry trends, technology, regulatory changes, and competitive landscape.

## What You’ll Do

- Define, validate, and communicate the product vision, goals, and roadmap.
- Write detailed product requirements, acceptance criteria, and wireframes when necessary.
- Guide new features from ideation, through delivery, to post-launch analysis.
- Collaborate with Engineering and Design on solution design, tradeoffs, and timelines.
- Partner with QA to ensure high-quality product releases.
- Conduct customer interviews, usability testing, and continuous research.
- Evangelize the product both inside and outside the company.
- Own OKRs and ensure alignment across all stakeholders.

## Required Skills and Experience

- 5+ years experience in product management, ideally for SaaS or e-commerce products.
- Demonstrated success managing all aspects of a product lifecycle.
- Experience working with cross-functional teams in an Agile environment.
- Strong analytical skills, including proficiency in data-driven decision making.
- Ability to articulate and evangelize product strategy, rationale, and results.
- Strong UX and design sensibilities; able to partner productively with designers.
- Excellent communication, organizational, and leadership skills.
- Experience creating and analyzing metrics, dashboards, and KPIs.
- Familiarity with product management, analytics, and collaboration tools (e.g., Jira, Figma, Mixpanel).

## Nice to Have

- Experience with large-scale B2B SaaS or multi-tenant platforms.
- Knowledge of enterprise e-commerce requirements and trends.
- Technical acumen; ability to work productively with engineers and understand technical tradeoffs.
- Background in user research or UX testing.
- Experience launching APIs or platform products.

## Success Criteria

- Delivery of a product roadmap and execution aligned with business goals.
- Measurable improvements in product metrics (adoption, retention, NPS, etc.).
- Positive stakeholder and customer feedback.
- Smooth collaboration and clear communication across teams.
