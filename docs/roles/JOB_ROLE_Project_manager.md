# Senior Project Manager

## About the Role

As a Senior Project Manager, you will be responsible for planning, executing, and closing complex projects for the enterprise e-commerce platform. You will coordinate cross-functional teams, define project goals and requirements, manage timelines and resources, mitigate risks, and ensure successful delivery in alignment with business objectives. You’ll act as the main point of contact between leadership, stakeholders, and delivery teams, ensuring clear communication and adherence to quality standards throughout the project lifecycle.

The ideal candidate demonstrates strong leadership, organization, and communication skills, with a proven record of successful project delivery within fast-paced, technology-driven environments.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define project objectives, scope, deliverables, and success criteria in collaboration with stakeholders.
- Develop detailed project plans, including timelines, resource allocation, milestones, and budgets.
- Proactively manage changes in project scope, identify potential risks, and implement mitigation plans.
- Coordinate efforts across Engineering, Product, Design, QA, Support, and external vendors as needed.
- Lead and motivate multidisciplinary teams to meet project goals and deadlines.
- Organize and facilitate project meetings, status updates, sprint planning, retrospectives, and reviews.
- Track progress, resolve issues, and communicate project status to stakeholders at all levels.
- Ensure project documentation, requirements, and deliverables are complete, current, and stored appropriately.
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and facilitate retrospectives to identify and implement best practices.
- Ensure all projects are delivered on-time, within scope and budget, and meet required quality standards.

## What You’ll Do

- Manage all aspects of assigned projects, from initiation through closure.
- Establish and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders.
- Break down complex initiatives into actionable workstreams with clear dependencies.
- Monitor team workload, remove blockers, and optimize resource utilization.
- Utilize project management and collaboration tools (e.g., Jira, Trello, Confluence, Slack).
- Prepare regular executive reports, dashboards, and project summaries.
- Perform post-project analysis to inform process improvements.

## Required Skills and Experience

- 5+ years of project management experience in software/IT, ideally for SaaS or e-commerce solutions.
- Proven success leading full lifecycle software projects in an Agile, Scrum, or hybrid setting.
- Experience managing cross-functional teams across multiple time zones.
- Excellent communication, interpersonal, and negotiation skills.
- Strong problem-solving and organizational abilities; able to handle multiple priorities simultaneously.
- Demonstrated ability to identify, track, and mitigate project risks effectively.
- Proficiency with project management software (Jira, Trello, Asana, MS Project, or similar).
- Ability to create actionable project plans, Gantt charts, and status reports.
- Comfortable working in a rapidly evolving, high-growth environment.

## Nice to Have

- PMP, Agile, or Scrum Master certification.
- Experience with large-scale software migrations, platform launches, or third-party integrations.
- Familiarity with software engineering fundamentals, product management, and UX best practices.
- Experience with remote, distributed, or global teams.
- Experience in client-facing project delivery or agency environments.

## Success Criteria

- Projects delivered on-time, within budget, and meeting agreed-upon scope and quality.
- Proactive identification and management of project risks/issues.
- High levels of stakeholder satisfaction and team morale.
- Transparent, predictable project tracking and communication.
- Continuous improvement of project management practices and processes.
