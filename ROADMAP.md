# Roadmap

## Short Term
- Add product price as a top-level schema field
- Product search and category filters
- Order list UI and order details page
- Address collection and shipping methods
- Coupon/discount support

## Medium Term
- Inventory management (stock counts)
- Admin dashboard for products/orders/users
- Webhooks to verify payment events from Stripe
- Email notifications (order confirmations)

## Long Term
- Multi-currency pricing
- Subscriptions (Stripe) for recurring products
- Performance and SEO improvements (SSR or Next.js)
